# AUTOPULSE
SENTIMENT ANALYSIS OF USED CAR REVIEWS 

INTRODUCTION:-
Nowadays, many people buy used cars from online platforms like Cars24 and Spinny. Before buying a car, customers
read reviews to understand the quality of vehicles and the services provided. However, reading thousands of reviews is
time-consuming. In this project, we collect customer reviews from both platforms and preprocess the text using Natural
Language Processing (NLP) techniques such as tokenization, stop-word removal, lemmatization, and text cleaning. We
then use deep learning models such as LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory), Bi-LSTM (Bidirectional Long Short-Term
Memory), or BERT (Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers) to classify each review as Positive,
Negative, or Neutral. The system also performs aspect-based analysis to identify common topics such as price, car
condition, delivery, documentation, mileage, and customer service. Furthermore, we compare the performance of
Cars24 and Spinny based on customer opinions and sentiment scores. Finally, the results are displayed in an interactive
dashboard using charts and graphs. This helps customers quickly understand which platform has better customer
satisfaction and enables companies to identify customer concerns and improve their services.

• The popularity of online used car platforms such as Cars24 and Spinny has increased significantly in recent
years.
• Customer reviews play a vital role in helping buyers evaluate vehicle quality, pricing, delivery, documentation,
and customer service.
• Manually analyzing thousands of customer reviews is time-consuming and difficult.
• This project uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Deep Learning techniques to automatically analyze
customer reviews.
• The system identifies customer opinions on key aspects, including price, vehicle condition, mileage, delivery,
documentation, and customer service.
